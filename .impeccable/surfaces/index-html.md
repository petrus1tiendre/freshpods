# Surface brief: index.html (FreshPads landing page)

Scope: the single public page of freshpads.ca. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: francophone Canadian owners of premium over-ear headphones with greyed, stained cushions; on a phone, already convinced home washing is risky.
Job: book a cushion cleaning. Action: the booking form on the page (name, contact, headset model, handoff).
Proof: Hans's own photos of the dirty cushions (2026-11-07) and the cleaned headset; a synthetic "after" derived from the before photo, labeled as such until a real after in the same framing exists.
Constraints: French first; no "AirPods"/"Apple" in name or logo; price, city, handoff, turnaround unknown and shown as "à confirmer", never invented. User-pinned quality bar: Apple / SpaceX product-page craft (full-bleed product, big quiet type, scroll-driven reveal). Decision round skipped at the user's explicit request ("vas-y, construis… ta propre intelligence"); the roll's assignment was still honored.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the page is the cushion seen once, very large, getting clean under the visitor's own scroll. It refuses the category's split hero (copy left, product right, badge row) and the three-icon-cards service page.

OWN-WORLD: anodized-aluminium service manual. Ground: brushed light silver (#e9eaec with hairline brushing), surfaces slightly lighter (#f4f5f6); ink: engraved charcoal (#1b1d20); secondary ink tinted from the metal (#5c6066). One accent, ice-mint (#19c7a8), confined to hairlines, the scroll progress rail and the "Après" marker; never text. Type: Familjen Grotesk for display (tight, 500–600), Hanken Grotesk for body. Components: engraved labels (small caps-height sans, 0.08em tracking), hairline rules, one charcoal pill button, oval "porthole" frames shaped like the cushion. Icons: authored SVG, 1.5px stroke.
Raises: from the iridescent-cloud-edge challenger, colour confined to hairline edges while the text field stays achromatic; from the airport-signage challenger, one message per band, each section says one thing; from the nixie-counter challenger, quantities are set as engraved values (price, delay) rather than bullet text.

STORY: visitor recognises their own grey cushions → scrolls and watches them turn white → understands it is a cleaning, not a replacement → sees the protocol and real proof → reads the (to-be-confirmed) price and handoff → books.

FIRST VIEWPORT: wordmark top-left, "Réserver" pill top-right. Headline across the top in two lines, display 6rem desktop / 2.6rem mobile: "Vos coussinets, / blancs comme au premier jour." Below it, centred, an oval porthole ~68vh tall shaped like a cushion, holding the dirty-cushion photo; a small engraved "AVANT" marker at its edge. The primary action, a charcoal pill "Réserver un nettoyage", sits overlapping the oval's bottom edge. One line under it: "Compatible AirPods Max et casques à coussinets en maille."

FORM: pinned scroll porthole (candidate 4 of 7 on the ordered grounded list: aluminium service manual) · seed:072ace37176e7fab.
