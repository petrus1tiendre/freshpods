## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS (one page, no build step), so it deploys anywhere (Cloudflare Pages, Vercel) and stays cheap to iterate. Chosen by Claude after Hans delegated the v1 (2026-11-07).

## Users

[inferred] Owners of premium over-ear headphones (AirPods Max and similar mesh-cushion headsets) whose ear cushions have greyed, stained or started to smell from daily wear. They are in Canada, mostly francophone (Québec / Outaouais), on their phone, and they have already searched "how to clean AirPods Max cushions" and found that washing them at home ruins the fabric or the foam. Job: get the cushions looking and smelling new again without buying $90 replacements.

## Product Purpose

FreshPods is a cushion-cleaning service for premium headphones. The owner hands over the headset (or just the cushions, which clip off magnetically), FreshPods deep-cleans the mesh fabric and foam, and returns them white and fresh. Success: the customer books a cleaning, gets the pads back looking like the "after" photos, and recommends the service.

## Positioning

Cleaning instead of replacing: a cushion-specific process that restores the original cushions, cheaper than official replacement pads and without the waste. The proof is the owner's own before/after photos of the same cushions.
